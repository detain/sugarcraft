## Goal

There are today **three** glob dialects in `candy-code` that all answer the question "does this path match
this pattern": `PathTrigger::pattern()` (`src/Context/Triggers/PathTrigger.php:143-178`),
`SkillRegistry::pathMatches()` (`src/Skills/SkillRegistry.php:650-790`), and `legacyPathMatch()`
(`:628`, the PCRE-failure fallback) — a **third** dialect, not a copy of either.

Reduce this to **ONE compiler implementing the SkillRegistry dialect**, with both production matchers routed
through it. `legacyPathMatch()` stays reachable exactly as it is today (it is the documented fallback when a
pattern fails to compile) — **it is not deleted, not stubbed, not narrowed** (§1.10); its divergence is
recorded, not silently absorbed.

## Why the SkillRegistry dialect, and not the stricter shell one (RULED — do not re-open)

1. **It is the plan's intent.** `prompt_plan.md:2371` gives the motivating example as
   "`*/tests/**/*.php` wherever they live". Premise check Q3 row **#26** measured `a/b/tests/FooTest.php`
   against exactly that pattern: `PathTrigger` = **no**, `SkillRegistry` = **yes**. "Wherever they live" is
   the SR answer. The plan's own example does not work under the stricter compiler.
2. **Non-narrowing is repo law.** Unifying the other way would turn off capability that exists today: a
   skill whose `paths:` says `src/*.php` currently announces for `src/deep/x.php` (row #6). Making `*`
   segment-scoped would silently stop that announcement. Widening `PathTrigger` removes nothing.
3. **The cost is asymmetric.** `PathTrigger` has **no production reader at all** (Q2: nothing consumes
   `Rule->triggers()` yet — the only references are `Rule.php:313` self-carry and a comment at
   `Runtime.php:2561`). So changing it affects no shipped behaviour. `SkillRegistry`'s matcher is live.
4. **Product consistency.** Every other user-facing `paths:` in this product is a `SKILL.md` frontmatter
   field read by `SkillRegistry::pathMatches()`. A rule author copying a working `paths:` value from a
   SKILL.md into a rule must not get a different language.

Record all four reasons in the new matcher's doc-block, with the row numbers, so the next reader does not
"fix" the looseness back. `PathTrigger.php:46-51` and `Rule.php:41-51` both currently DEFER this decision to
the orchestrator — after your step they must point at the resolved dialect instead, discharging F-PATHDIALECT
rather than re-flagging it.
