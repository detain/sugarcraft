**B. The assemble invariant** — `Runtime::assembleSections()` and `CompleteRequest`:
`assembleSections()` is a ONE-fold producing `[$systemPrompt, $systemBlocks]`; the string arm
and the block arm come from the same accumulator, so `implode('', $systemBlocks) === $systemPrompt`
holds BY CONSTRUCTION (byte-for-byte), not by convention. `Runtime::run()` destructures that fold
into `new CompleteRequest(systemPrompt:…, systemBlocks:…)`.
